# Chamomile Flaxseed Hair Gel (with Vegetable Glycerin)

**Yield:** About 1–1.5 cups (enough for several uses on long hair)  
**Shelf life:** 7–14 days in the fridge

## Ingredients
- 2–3 tablespoons whole flax seeds
- 1.5–2 cups water
- 3–4 tablespoons dried chamomile flowers (or 5–6 chamomile tea bags)
- **2 teaspoons vegetable glycerin** (higher end of recommended range)
- 1–3 teaspoons apple cider vinegar (ACV)
- **Optional:** 1–2 tbsp aloe vera gel/juice (extra hydration)
- **Optional:** 2–4 drops rosemary or lavender essential oil (scent + scalp benefits)

## Instructions
1. In a saucepan, combine the water, flax seeds, and chamomile (use flowers or tea bags).
2. Bring to a boil over medium heat, then reduce to a gentle simmer for 8–12 minutes. Stir occasionally until the mixture thickens to a gel-like consistency (it will continue to thicken as it cools).
3. Remove from heat, cover the pot, and let it steep for an additional 10–15 minutes for maximum chamomile extraction.
4. Strain the mixture through a fine mesh strainer or cheesecloth into a clean bowl. Press or squeeze firmly to extract as much liquid as possible. Discard the solids.
5. While the gel is still slightly warm, stir in the **2 teaspoons of vegetable glycerin** until fully mixed.
6. Allow it to cool slightly, then mix in the apple cider vinegar. Add any optional ingredients (aloe or essential oils) at this stage.
7. Transfer the finished gel to a clean jar or squeeze bottle. Place it in the refrigerator to cool completely (it will thicken further).

## How to Use
- After showering, apply a generous amount to **damp hair**.
- Rake through sections or scrunch to encourage curl definition and even distribution.
- Air dry or diffuse as usual.
- Shake or stir the jar before each use (natural separation is normal).

**Tips for best results:**
- 2 tsp vegetable glycerin is at the higher end of the recommended range for good moisture without excess stickiness.
- If the gel feels too thick, thin it with a splash of water when applying.
- For extra brightening, use this gel after your chamomile rinse.
- Clarify your hair occasionally if you notice any buildup.

**Storage:** Keep refrigerated. Use within 1–2 weeks. Discard if it develops an off smell or mold.
